ATLAS packs Tuesday 24 November 2026 into a single 1024x1024 image: a giant date masthead, a UTC timeline and a day-in-numbers strip lead into four illustrated leads — the Huawei guilty verdict, the Hormuz tankers, Ukraine's strikes on Russian ships, and the Cartagena hostages. Ten checked tier-2 stories carry verification tags, a numbered-pin world map places the day's geography at a glance, twenty briefs fill out the picture, and a date-check strip names older stories deliberately excluded.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124

## Run

`render.py` (PIL, local only) draws `entry.png`. `main.py` is stdlib-only and copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
